Questions

Before you walk in

Do I need a finished idea?

No. A problem you care about is enough to start. Shaping the idea is part of the Lab.

I'm a student — can I join?

Yes. Students are exactly who the Lab is for. Tell us about yourself and your idea using the form on the Join page.

Do I have to be in Indore?

Walkover works remotely, so you can walk with us from anywhere. Our home base is LIC Tower, Indore.

What happens after I share my idea?

The team reads every idea. If there's a fit, someone from Walkover will reach out to talk about next steps.
